Responsibly sourced. Properly built.

Every piece of timber I use is sourced from FSC-certified merchants, the Forest Stewardship Council standard that guarantees the wood comes from responsibly managed forests. No illegal logging, no habitat destruction, no greenwashing.

Beyond sourcing, building things properly is itself the sustainable choice. A deck or fence built correctly lasts fifteen to twenty years. A cheap job that fails in five means double the material use, double the waste, double the cost to you.

Built right the first time is the greenest thing I can do.

How I source timber

Five steps. No surprises.

  1. 1

    First conversation

    Call or WhatsApp to talk through your project. No hard sell, no obligation, just a straightforward chat about what you have in mind and whether I'm the right person for the job.

  2. 2

    Site visit and design

    I come to your garden, take measurements, and talk through the design with you in person. We finalise exactly what you want: timber or composite, dimensions, style, finish.

  3. 3

    Your quote

    You get a detailed, honest quote. It details all the work to be carried out and includes the payment structure.

  4. 4

    The build

    I arrive when I say I will, keep the site clean, and keep you updated throughout. If anything comes up mid-job, I talk to you directly, not around you.

  5. 5

    Final walkthrough

    Before I leave, we walk the finished job together. You're satisfied with every detail, or we sort it on the spot. That's the standard, every time.
